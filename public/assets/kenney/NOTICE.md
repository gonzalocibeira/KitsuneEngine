# Kenney Tiny Asset Packs

This directory contains selected files from two Kenney asset packs:

- Tiny Dungeon 1.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon
- Tiny Town 1.1: https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town
- RPG Urban Pack 1.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-urban-pack

These packs are distributed by Kenney under Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Attribution is not required, but credit to Kenney or https://kenney.nl is appreciated.

Included files:

- `tiny-dungeon/Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png`
- `tiny-dungeon/Tilemap/tilemap.png`
- `tiny-dungeon/License.txt`
- `tiny-dungeon/Tilesheet.txt`
- `tiny-town/Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png`
- `tiny-town/Tilemap/tilemap.png`
- `tiny-town/License.txt`
- `tiny-town/Tilesheet.txt`
- `rpg-urban-pack/Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png`
- `rpg-urban-pack/Tilemap/tilemap.png`
- `rpg-urban-pack/Tilemap/tilemap.txt`
- `rpg-urban-pack/License.txt`
